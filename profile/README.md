## 🧠 Overview

Slay the Spire 2 is a roguelike deckbuilding game developed by Mega Crit and released in Early Access on March 5, 2026. The sequel combines strategic card combat, procedurally changing runs, character progression, powerful relics, and new encounters as players climb the mysterious Spire. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

The game can be played as a single-player experience or through online co-op for up to four players. Slay the Spire 2 introduces new and returning characters, cards, relics, enemies, events, environments, and gameplay mechanics. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

## Additional Information for Windows Users

Slay the Spire 2 is currently an Early Access PC game, with the Windows version available through Steam alongside macOS and Linux versions. The developers state that additional cards, events, environments, enemies, mechanics, balance adjustments, and other content are planned throughout Early Access.

The game is designed around repeatable runs, strategic deckbuilding, card combinations, relic selection, procedural encounters, and character-specific mechanics. This makes each ascent an opportunity to experiment with different builds and strategies.

Slay the Spire 2 also introduces online cooperative play for up to four players. The developers have confirmed Steam Workshop support as part of the game's development plans. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}
